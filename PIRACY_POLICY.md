Intelligent Email Assistant ("we," "us," "our," or "the Company") is committed to protecting intellectual property rights and combating piracy. This Piracy Policy outlines our stance on intellectual property protection, our efforts to prevent unauthorized use of our software, and the consequences of piracy.

## 2. What Constitutes Piracy

Piracy includes, but is not limited to:

- **Unauthorized Distribution**: Sharing, distributing, or uploading our software without proper authorization
- **Software Counterfeiting**: Creating, selling, or distributing counterfeit copies of our application
- **License Violations**: Using our service beyond the scope of your license or without a valid license
- **Source Code Theft**: Unauthorized copying, modification, or distribution of our source code
- **Credential Sharing**: Sharing login credentials, API keys, or authentication tokens across multiple unauthorized users
- **Unauthorized Modifications**: Altering, reverse-engineering, or decompiling our software for redistribution
- **Circumventing Protection Measures**: Bypassing or removing copyright notices, license keys, or access controls
- **Unauthorized Commercial Use**: Using our service for commercial purposes without proper licensing
